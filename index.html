ABOUT THE PROJECT

A simple solution for better study habits.

The Daily Routine Study Planner is an Android application developed to help students manage their daily academic schedules efficiently.

The application combines task scheduling, reminders, study materials and educational resources into a single platform, making students' daily learning activities more organized and accessible.
